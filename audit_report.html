🔴 1. Critical Technical & SEO Fixes (Implemented)

1.1 Broken Canonicals on `/privacy` and `/terms`

Both legal pages incorrectly pointed their canonical link to the homepage, signaling to search engines that they were redundant duplicates. Fixed: Canonical tags are now self-referencing on both routes.

1.2 Stagnant Open Graph Data Across All Product Routes

Product pages (`/birthday`, `/proposal`, `/sorry`, `/anniversary`) outputted generic homepage metadata. WhatsApp and Instagram shares showed generic copy instead of the actual card context. Fixed: Every experience now injects custom `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:url`, and tailored social preview cards.

1.3 Missing Social Image on `/blog`

The blog was missing `og:image` and `twitter:image`. Fixed: Added high-resolution 1200×630 Open Graph fallback images.

1.4 Dead Anchor Nav Links on Subpages

The navigation on `/blog` pointed to relative hashes (`#experiences`, `#how`, `#stories`), resulting in dead clicks. Fixed: All subpage navigation headers now target root anchors (`index.html#experiences`, `index.html#how`, `index.html#stories`).
