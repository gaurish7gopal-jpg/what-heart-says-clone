Our Story

Some things deserve more than a text.

Paper cards get left in drawers. WhatsApp text paragraphs get skimmed and forgotten. We built What Heart Says to occupy the rare space in between: deeply personal, interactive digital moments made specifically for their phone screen.

Over 10,000+ people across India and the globe have used our experiences to apologize tenderly, celebrate birthdays with flickering candles they blow out themselves, ask the biggest question of their lives with an unrejectable proposal card, and mark anniversary milestones with animated love meters.
