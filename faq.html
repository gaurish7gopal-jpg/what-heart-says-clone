Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about creating, sharing, and viewing interactive digital cards.

✨ How does What Heart Says work?

You pick an experience (Birthday, Proposal, Sorry, or Anniversary), type your recipient's name and heartfelt words in 2 minutes, preview the whole card for free, pay ₹199, and share the secret link via WhatsApp or Instagram. They tap, the experience opens on their phone screen with zero friction.

👀 Can I preview the card before paying?

Yes, absolutely! You can play through every animation, candle blow-out, dodging button, and letter reveal end-to-end completely free before spending a single rupee.

🛡️ What is your refund policy?

We offer a 100% money-back refund within 24 hours of purchase as long as the recipient has not opened the link. If you entered a typo or made an error, reach out to support@whatheartsays.com and we will assist right away.

📱 Does the recipient need an app?

No app is required! The card opens directly inside their mobile browser (Safari, Chrome, WhatsApp in-app browser). It runs silky smooth on iPhone and Android devices.

🏷️ How much does it cost?

Every card is priced at a straightforward ₹199 with no hidden subscriptions or upsells. The link replays forever.
