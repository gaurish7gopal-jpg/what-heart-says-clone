Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026 · Operated by Zenaclub Tech (OPC) Private Limited

At What Heart Says (operated by Zenaclub Tech (OPC) Private Limited, Faridabad, Haryana), we respect your privacy and take protecting personal emotions and memories seriously.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an interactive card experience, we collect:

  • Recipient's name and sender's chosen moniker.
  • Your customized messages, letters, and celebration dates.
  • Transaction identification data processed via verified secure payment gateways. We never store raw credit card numbers or banking passwords.

2. How Your Cards are Stored

Every card generated receives an encrypted, randomized shortcode. The card content is strictly accessible only to people possessing the direct link or shortcode.

3. Security & Payments

All payments are securely processed through encrypted RBI-compliant payment gateways. Our checkout complies with industry standard encryption (TLS 1.3).

4. Contact Us

For any grievances or questions regarding this Privacy Policy, write to our grievance officer at support@whatheartsays.com.
